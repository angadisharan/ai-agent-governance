# Security Review Checklist

- [ ] Trust boundaries and sensitive assets are identified.
- [ ] Authentication and authorisation are enforced server-side.
- [ ] Tenant and resource isolation are verified.
- [ ] No secrets or private data are hardcoded or logged.
- [ ] Inputs, uploads, callbacks, and external data are validated.
- [ ] Rate limits, timeouts, and abuse controls are appropriate.
- [ ] Dependencies and licences are reviewed.
- [ ] Errors do not reveal internal details.
- [ ] Data retention, privacy, and residency requirements are addressed.
- [ ] Threats and mitigations are documented.
- [ ] High-impact unresolved risks are escalated.
